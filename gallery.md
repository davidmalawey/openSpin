## Videos
Starting with one video, demonstrating the value of our printable designs.  One key point is **flexibility** over performance.  The COTS bearings handle more speed, load, and durability but our printable designs have a different value: they enable designers to pivot the design into new creations, like turning this bearing into a slip-ring.  Slip rings transmit electrical power while the joint spins unlimited rotations.  They bypass our need for cords, so robots and mechanisms can be made to do brand new things.

The next video gives a quick demonstration of testing for the bearing with nylon balls.  At this stage (2023) my design is far from optimized, and by 2025 many designers have shared newer designs that may have higher strength or better tolerances.  Then why would I design my own?  Because we need the digital backbone for our parts in the ecosystem to be fully documented, and this is what we can't find most of the time.  I've treated this bearing as a working placeholder - it's not the best in the world but it offers accessibility to other experts to form improvements, and capture those improvements in a structured way through documentation.  **ACCESSIBILITY for EXPERTS through DOCUMENTATION.**  That's a big idea to keep in mind.
